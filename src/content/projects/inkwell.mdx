# Inkwell

Inkwell is for finding books and keeping track of what you're reading. You put books on shelves (the defaults or your own), log page progress toward a daily reading goal, rate and review what you finish, and follow friends to see what they're up to. Book pages show similar books and a rating chart, and there's a Goodreads importer that brings over your shelves and reading history without overwriting anything already in Inkwell.

The data is split in two. Profiles, shelves, and reading activity live in Convex, while the catalog of books, authors, series, genres, and ISBNs moved out into its own Postgres database. Covers are served from R2, falling back to Open Library, then Wikipedia, and finally a generated cover with the title and author on it.

The web app is TanStack Start, and there's an Expo companion app that reads from the same catalog.
